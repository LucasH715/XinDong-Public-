# 薪动 | XinDong

### 让收入增长看得见。
**Make Every Moment Count.**

薪动（XinDong）是一款简洁、直观的实时薪资计时工具。

它将工作时间转化为持续变化的收入数字，让每一秒的努力都变得可见。

XinDong is a simple and intuitive real-time earnings tracker that transforms your working hours into visible income growth.

## ✨ 主要功能 | Features

### 💰 实时薪资计算
根据设定的薪资与工作时间，实时计算收入增长。

**Real-time Earnings Tracking**

Watch your earnings grow based on your salary settings and working hours.

### ⏱️ 工作时间可视化
将抽象的工作收入转化为直观、持续更新的数字。

**Visualized Working Time**

Turn working hours into a clear and continuously updating earnings display.

### 🌍 多货币支持
支持人民币、美元、加元、澳元等常见货币。

**Multiple Currencies**

Support for major currencies including CNY, USD, CAD, and AUD.

### 🎨 个性化外观
提供深色与浅色主题，以及可自定义的界面选项。

**Customizable Appearance**

Choose between light and dark themes with personalized display options.

### 🌐 多语言界面
支持简体中文与英语。

**Multilingual Interface**

Available in Simplified Chinese and English.

### 📱 多平台体验
针对不同设备提供相应的实时收入展示方式。

**Multi-platform Experience**

Designed to provide convenient earnings tracking across supported devices.
